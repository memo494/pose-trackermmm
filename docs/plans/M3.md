## Consult C1 (answered by the lead; owner on 2026-11-15: "you are the consultant", no separate agent)
**Question.** Do the §6.3 lifecycle numbers (birth at score ≥ 0.6, confirmed after 3 hits in 300 ms, only
confirmed tracks drawn) fit a screen-capture source?
**Evidence.** `docs/plans/M0c.md` "Measured after T1": real people in the owner's video score 0.34 to 0.68 at 640
(walker 0.35, man on the ground 0.59, bending person 0.15 to 0.68 with scale). Today every detection ≥ 0.15 is
marked at once. Capture delivers about 20 fps while the video moves and no frames on a static screen.
**Answer.** Birth at 0.6 would leave most of his people without a mark: a regression. Decisions:
1. **One time rule.** Every Kalman step and every lifecycle clock uses `dtEff = min(real dt, maxDtMs)`,
   `maxDtMs` = 100. A gap without frames therefore adds at most 100 ms once: it cannot age or kill a track.
   A step with `real dt > maxDtMs` also inflates the process noise ×10 for that step (the gate opens).
2. **Scores.** `trackHighScore` 0.5 (stage 1), low = `scoreThreshold` 0.15 (stage 2, existing tracks only),
   `trackBirthScore` **0.25** (an unmatched detection at or above it starts a tentative track).
3. **Shown tracks.** A track is shown from its **3rd consecutive hit** (`trackConfirmHits` 3; about 100 ms at
   20 fps). A tentative track dies on its first miss, so a one- or two-frame false positive is never shown,
   whatever its score. No "instant show" for high scores: one rule, and the delay is not visible.
4. **Still picture.** `onIdle(nowNs)`: a tentative track that was hit on the newest frame and has seen no frame
   for `trackIdlePromoteMs` (400) becomes confirmed and shown. The app calls it from the HUD tick.
5. **Lost tracks.** A confirmed track that misses coasts on its prediction (velocity decays, τ 100 ms) and stays
   shown until `maxLostMs` 800 of `dtEff` time. It is deleted at once when its predicted head point leaves the
   image by more than `trackExitMarginRatio` (0.1) × its height.
6. **Dedupe before association:** of two detections with IoU ≥ `dedupeIou` (0.7) the lower score is dropped.
7. **Kalman:** three independent position/velocity filters (head x, head y, box height), discrete white-noise
   acceleration, §6.3 noise scaling. No matrix library, no allocation.

**Thresholds are grounded in a simulation** (lead, 2026-11-15; σ_acc 8h/s², σ_meas 0.03h, keypoint noise 0.01h on
five head keypoints, h = 200 px, 20 seeds): still jitter (RMS of the 2nd difference) tracked/raw = 0.63 at
20 fps, 0.44 at 30, 0.22 at 60; constant-velocity error 0.03 to 0.06 frames of travel; overshoot after an abrupt
stop from 2h/s 0.036h to 0.050h. So the Kalman alone does **not** reach the §9 "< 1 px" at 20 fps (1.37 px);
that absolute number belongs to M4 (OneEuro on top). M3 asserts the ratios below.
**Confidence:** high for 1, 3, 5, 6, 7; medium for the two score numbers (tuned on the phone in M4's sheet).

## M3a — the tracker in `core` (package `core/tracking`, pure Kotlin, JUnit 5)
All sizes relative to the person's box height `h` (Img px). No allocation per update after construction
(preallocated arrays sized by `TrackerConfig.maxDetections` and a new `maxTracks`). Every number is a
`TrackerConfig` field with KDoc, unit and bounds. Deterministic: same input, same ids.

### T1 — Synthetic scenes and metrics (test code + their own tests) · M
`core/src/test/.../tracking/{SyntheticScene,TrackingMetrics}.kt` and tests.
- `SyntheticScene`: people with piecewise-linear head paths and box heights; per frame it emits
  `DetectionFrame`s (box around the head: left/right = head x ∓ 0.2h, top = head y − 0.1h, bottom = top + h;
  17 keypoints, the five head keypoints at fixed offsets of the head centre, others anywhere inside the box);
  seeded Gaussian noise per keypoint and box edge; per-person score as a function of time; per-person dropouts;
  keypoint-confidence dropouts; false positives; frame times from fps with optional jitter and idle gaps.
- `TrackingMetrics` on (truth, shown tracks per frame): `idSwitches`, `coverage` (share of frames a person has a
  shown track within 0.5h), `ghostFrames`, `jitterRms` (RMS of the 2nd difference), `overshoot`, `lagFrames`.
- Tests of the tools themselves on hand-built track lists (a perfect follower → 0 switches, coverage 1; a
  swapped pair → 2 switches; known jitter and lag values).

### T2 — `HeadPointEstimator` · M
- Stateless `HeadMeasurement.of(detection, config)` for association: mean of the head keypoints 0-4 scoring at
  least `headKeypointEnterScore` (0.5), else the fallback `(box centre x, top + 0.10·h)` flagged `estimated`.
- Per track: hysteresis per keypoint (enter ≥ 0.5, leave < 0.3); EMA offsets of each visible head keypoint from
  the head centre in units of `h` (`headOffsetAlpha`); a missing keypoint is filled from its offset; the head
  centre is the confidence-weighted mean of the per-keypoint centre estimates; fallback as above until two
  keypoints have ever been visible. Measurement variance `(0.03·h)² / meanConf`, fallback `(0.10·h)²`.
- **Tests first:** all five visible → their mean; three of five vanish after learning → centre moves < 0.02h;
  hysteresis at 0.45 / 0.35 / 0.25; fallback flag and variance; scale change (h doubles) keeps the centre.

### T3 — `KalmanAxis` · S
Position/velocity filter for one axis: `predict(dtS, sigmaAccel, inflate)`, `update(z, variance)` returning
the innovation and its variance, `decayVelocity(dtS, tauS)`. Covariance stays symmetric and positive.
- **Tests first:** converges on constant velocity; two half steps equal one full step (predict only); miss
  decay halves the velocity after `τ·ln 2`; a huge innovation followed by `inflate` 10 re-converges faster than
  without; no NaN on `dt` = 0 (a repeated timestamp is a no-op).

### T4 — `Assigner`, association cost, dedupe · M
- `Assigner` interface + `HungarianAssigner` (rectangular cost matrix in a preallocated `FloatArray`,
  `FORBIDDEN` cost for gated pairs, ties broken by lower track index then lower detection index).
- `AssociationCost`: `0.5·(1 − IoU) + 0.5·min(1, dHead / (0.5·h))`; forbidden when the squared Mahalanobis
  distance of the head innovation exceeds `trackGateChi2` (9.21).
- `DetectionDedupe` (decision 6): stable, keeps the higher score, O(n²) on at most `maxDetections`.
- **Tests first:** known optimal assignments (3x3, 2x4, 4x2), all-forbidden rows stay unassigned, identical
  costs resolve the same way under row/column permutation, a brute-force check on random small matrices
  (seeded), dedupe cases (same person twice, two people side by side with IoU 0.3 both kept).
