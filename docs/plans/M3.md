## Consult C1 (answered by the lead; owner on 2026-11-15: "you are the consultant", no separate agent)
**Question.** Do the §6.3 lifecycle numbers (birth at score ≥ 0.6, confirmed after 3 hits in 300 ms, only
confirmed tracks drawn) fit a screen-capture source?
**Evidence.** `docs/plans/M0c.md` "Measured after T1": real people in the owner's video score 0.34 to 0.68 at 640
(walker 0.35, man on the ground 0.59, bending person 0.15 to 0.68 with scale). Today every detection ≥ 0.15 is
marked at once. Capture delivers about 20 fps while the video moves and no frames on a static screen.
**Answer.** Birth at 0.6 would leave most of his people without a mark: a regression. Decisions:
1. **One time rule.** Every Kalman step and every lifecycle clock uses `dtEff = min(real dt, maxDtMs)`,
   `maxDtMs` = 250 (was 100; C3). A gap without frames therefore adds at most that once: it cannot age or kill a
   track. A step with `real dt > maxDtMs` (a gap step) also inflates the process noise ×10 for that step.
2. **Scores.** `trackHighScore` 0.5 (stage 1), low = `scoreThreshold` 0.15 (stage 2, existing tracks only),
   `trackBirthScore` **0.25** (an unmatched detection at or above it starts a tentative track).
3. **Shown tracks.** A track is shown from its **3rd consecutive hit** (`trackConfirmHits` 3; about 100 ms at
   20 fps). A tentative track dies on its first miss, so a one- or two-frame false positive is never shown,
   whatever its score. No "instant show" for high scores: one rule, and the delay is not visible.
4. **Still picture.** `onIdle(nowNs)`: a tentative track that was hit on the newest frame and has seen no frame
   for `trackIdlePromoteMs` (400) becomes confirmed and shown. The app calls it from the HUD tick.
5. **Lost tracks.** A confirmed track that misses coasts on its prediction (velocity decays, τ 300 ms; C3) and stays
   shown until `maxLostMs` 800 of `dtEff` time. It is deleted at once when its predicted head point leaves the
   image by more than `trackExitMarginRatio` (0.1) × its height.
6. **Dedupe before association:** of two detections with IoU ≥ `dedupeIou` (0.7) the lower score is dropped.
7. **Kalman:** three independent position/velocity filters (head x, head y, box height), discrete white-noise
   acceleration, §6.3 noise scaling. No matrix library, no allocation.

**Thresholds are grounded in a simulation** (lead, 2026-11-15; σ_acc 8h/s², σ_meas 0.03h, keypoint noise 0.01h on
five head keypoints, h = 200 px, 20 seeds): still jitter (RMS of the 2nd difference) tracked/raw = 0.63 at
20 fps, 0.44 at 30, 0.22 at 60; constant-velocity error 0.03 to 0.06 frames of travel; overshoot after an abrupt
stop from 2h/s 0.036h to 0.050h. So the Kalman alone does **not** reach the §9 "< 1 px" at 20 fps (1.37 px);
that absolute number belongs to M4 (OneEuro on top). M3 asserts the ratios below.
**Confidence:** high for 1, 3, 5, 6, 7; medium for the two score numbers (tuned on the phone in M4's sheet).

## Consult C2 (lead on Fable 5.2, 2026-11-16) — what the assigner optimises
**Question.** T4 was started with "most pairs first, then least total cost" (forbidden pairs priced with a big
constant). Is that the right objective?
**Answer: no.** With it, when one person's detection is missing and a spare detection is near, three poor pairs
beat two good ones and the ids shift along a row of people (tracks A, B, C; detections a, b and a spare d next
to A: A-d, B-a, C-b instead of A-a, B-b). The rule is a **cost limit**, as in DeepSORT (costs clipped to
`max_distance`) and ByteTrack (`lapjv` with `cost_limit`): a pair is eligible when its cost is finite and below
`maxCost`, and the result maximises `sum(maxCost - cost)` over eligible pairs. A pair is only made when it is
worth it, and one good pair beats two poor ones. `maxCost` is a parameter of `assign`; the tracker passes one
limit per stage (T6). **Confidence:** high. Sent to the T4 implementer as a correction; in the code since `c97a2f8`.

## Consult C3 (lead on Fable 5.2, 2026-11-16) — the tracker's numbers, from a reference model
**Question.** Which values make the T5 scenarios hold at every frame rate, and are the §6.3 miss rules right?
**Method.** A Python model of the whole tracker (the T2–T4 code, the lifecycle of C1, `SyntheticScene`,
`TrackingMetrics`): `docs/plans/M3_sim/tracker_ref.py`. Every scenario ran at 20, 30 and 60 fps and at 30 fps
± 30 %, 40 to 100 seeds each (`scenarios.py`; the option studies are not kept). **Decisions:**
1. **Velocity decay on a miss: τ 100 → 300 ms.** With 100 ms and noise inflation on a miss, two people crossing
   slowly (0.3 h/s) in one row swapped ids in 9 to 16 % of the runs; with 300 ms and no inflation in about 1 %.
   A walker missing for 0.5 s is joined again up to 1.5 h/s instead of 1.0 h/s. Price: a lost track coasts about
   0.25 s of travel instead of 0.1 s.
2. **Noise ×10 only on a gap step.** Not on a miss (it made crossings worse; a lost track's covariance grows
   anyway) and not on "NIS above the gate" (§6.3 asks for both a gate and that rule; with the gate in the
   association a matched pair cannot exceed it). T7 corrects §6.3.
3. **`trackMaxDtMs` 100 → 250.** Steps up to 250 ms are ordinary (dropped frames, a device at 5 to 10 fps); with
   100 ms a 10 fps device made every other step a gap step (still-jitter ratio 0.85 instead of 0.78).
   **Limit:** a person faster than about 1 h/s whose picture freezes without a single frozen frame gets a new id
   afterwards. With 0.15 s of frozen frames first (a player's pause) 2 h/s keeps its id.
4. **Cost limits (C2):** stage 1 `trackMatchMaxCost` 0.7, stage 2 `trackLowMatchMaxCost` 0.6. For an equal box
   beside the track the cost is `s/(0.4+s) + s` (`s` = sideways distance in h), so 0.7 ≈ 0.28 h and 0.6 ≈ 0.23 h.
   Joining again after a dropout was the same from 0.6 to 0.9; a lost track took over a *new* person 0.3 h away
   in 0 to 10 % of the runs at 0.7 and in 93 to 100 % at 0.8.
5. **No matching cascade** (confirmed before tentative): no difference in any scenario, so one pool per stage.
6. **Height filter** `trackHeightAccelSigmaRatio` 2 h/s², `trackHeightNoiseRatio` 0.05; a new track starts with
   velocity σ `trackInitVelocitySigmaRatio` 2 h/s on all three axes. A person growing from 100 to 250 px in 3 s:
   head error ≤ 0.014 h.
7. **Crossing metric.** In the frame where two heads coincide `TrackingMetrics` may pair them the other way
   round; that is no id switch. S1 compares the ids before and after the overlap and counts switches outside it.
8. **`onIdle(idleNs)`** takes the time since the newest frame arrived, measured by the caller: no second clock
   base inside the tracker.
**Known limits (not asserted):** slow crossings in one row can still swap ids (no appearance model); a person
missing for 0.5 s while moving at 2 h/s gets a new id; flicker with more than about half of the frames missing.
**Confidence:** high for 2, 5, 7, 8; medium for 1, 3, 4, 6 (all `TrackerConfig` fields; tuned on the phone in M4).

### T2 — `HeadPointEstimator` · M
- Stateless `HeadMeasurement.of(detection, config)` for association: mean of the head keypoints 0-4 scoring at
  least `headKeypointEnterScore` (0.5), else the fallback `(box centre x, top + 0.10·h)` flagged `estimated`.
- Per track: hysteresis per keypoint (enter ≥ 0.5, leave < 0.3); EMA offsets of each visible head keypoint from
  the head centre in units of `h` (`headOffsetAlpha`); a missing keypoint is filled from its offset; the head
  centre is the confidence-weighted mean of the per-keypoint centre estimates; fallback as above until two
  keypoints have ever been visible. Measurement variance `(0.03·h)² / meanConf`, fallback `(0.10·h)²`.
- **Tests first:** all five visible → their mean; three of five vanish after learning → centre moves < 0.02h;
  hysteresis at 0.45 / 0.35 / 0.25; fallback flag and variance; scale change (h doubles) keeps the centre.

### T3 — `KalmanAxis` · S
Position/velocity filter for one axis: `predict(dtS, sigmaAccel, inflate)`, `update(z, variance)` returning
the innovation and its variance, `decayVelocity(dtS, tauS)`. Covariance stays symmetric and positive.
- **Tests first:** converges on constant velocity; two half steps equal one full step (predict only); miss
  decay halves the velocity after `τ·ln 2`; a huge innovation followed by `inflate` 10 re-converges faster than
  without; no NaN on `dt` = 0 (a repeated timestamp is a no-op).

### T4 — `Assigner`, association cost, dedupe · M
- `Assigner` interface + `HungarianAssigner` (rectangular cost matrix in a preallocated `FloatArray`,
  `FORBIDDEN` cost for gated pairs, ties broken by lower track index then lower detection index). Objective:
  the cost limit of Consult C2 (`assign(rows, cols, maxCost, rowToCol, colToRow)`), not "most pairs first".
- `AssociationCost`: `0.5·(1 − IoU) + 0.5·min(1, dHead / (0.5·h))`; forbidden when the squared Mahalanobis
  distance of the head innovation exceeds `trackGateChi2` (9.21).
- `DetectionDedupe` (decision 6): stable, keeps the higher score, O(n²) on at most `maxDetections`.
- **Tests first:** known optimal assignments (3x3, 2x4, 4x2), all-forbidden rows stay unassigned, identical
  costs resolve the same way under row/column permutation, a brute-force check on random small matrices
  (seeded), dedupe cases (same person twice, two people side by side with IoU 0.3 both kept).

    fun id(index: Int): Int
    fun state(index: Int): TrackState
    fun isShown(index: Int): Boolean                    // CONFIRMED or LOST
    fun headXImg(index: Int): Float; fun headYImg(index: Int): Float
    fun velocityXImgPerS(index: Int): Float; fun velocityYImgPerS(index: Int): Float

    fun isHeadEstimated(index: Int): Boolean            // newest head point was the box fallback
    fun lostMs(index: Int): Float                       // 0 unless LOST

class Tracker(config: TrackerConfig) {
    fun update(frame: DetectionFrame): TrackList
    fun onIdle(idleNs: Long): TrackList                 // time since the newest frame arrived (C3.8)
    fun reset()
}

### T6 — `Tracker` · L
Port `Tracker.update`, `on_idle` and the track state of `docs/plans/M3_sim/tracker_ref.py` (about 70 lines)
onto the T2–T4 classes; **where this text and the model differ, the model wins: report the difference.**
1. Time: first frame, no predict. `dt` from the frame timestamps; `dt ≤ 0` → the frame is ignored (list
   unchanged). `dtEff = min(dt, trackMaxDtMs)`; a gap step (`dt > trackMaxDtMs`) uses `trackGapNoiseInflate`.
2. Predict every track with `dtEff`: head x and y with σ `trackAccelSigmaRatio · h`, height with
   `trackHeightAccelSigmaRatio · h` (`h` = the track's height before the step).
3. Detections: at most `MAX_DETECTIONS_PER_FRAME` (never throw on more), `DetectionDedupe`, drop scores below
   `scoreThreshold` and invalid `HeadMeasurement`s; high = score ≥ `trackHighScore`.
4. Stage 1: all tracks × high, `AssociationCost` (track box = predicted head + stored box offsets × predicted
   height; innovation variance = `pPos` + measurement variance), `assign` with `trackMatchMaxCost`. Stage 2: the
   unmatched tracks × low with `trackLowMatchMaxCost`.
5. Matched: `HeadPointEstimator.update` → point; x and y `update(point, its variance)`; height
   `update(box height, (trackHeightNoiseRatio · predicted height)²)`; box offsets = (edges − point) / box height;
   hits + 1, `lostMs` 0; TENTATIVE with `trackConfirmHits` hits → CONFIRMED; LOST → CONFIRMED.
6. Unmatched: TENTATIVE → removed. Else LOST: `lostMs += dtEff`; the three velocities × `exp(−dtEff / τ)`;
   removed when `lostMs > trackMaxLostMs` or the head point is outside the image by more than
   `trackExitMarginRatio` × height. A track whose height estimate is not finite and positive is removed.
7. Births, in detection order, for unmatched detections with score ≥ `trackBirthScore` while fewer than
   `maxTracks` tracks exist: next id; estimator reset and update; filters reset with the point's variance
   (height: `(trackHeightNoiseRatio · h)²`) and velocity variance `(trackInitVelocitySigmaRatio · h)²`.
8. `onIdle(idleNs)`: at or above `trackIdlePromoteMs` every TENTATIVE track becomes CONFIRMED; nothing else.
   `reset()`: no tracks, no last timestamp; the id counter goes on (ids start at 1, never reused).
9. Counters for the diagnostics of M3b, cumulative since construction (`reset()` keeps them): `birthCount`,
   `confirmCount` (by hits), `idlePromoteCount`, `recoverCount` (LOST → CONFIRMED), `removeCount` (confirmed
   tracks only) and `mostShownAtOnce`.

## M3b — the tracker in the app (branch `m3b-tracker-app`, one PR; first change the owner sees)
Read `AGENTS.md`, ARCHITECTURE §5 (lines 183-221) and §6.5 (312-342), and the task. Paths below are under
`app/src/main/kotlin/com/memo494/posetracker/` unless they start with `core/`.

**Design (lead).**
- The tracker lives in `FramePipeline` and is touched only on the capture thread. The overlay never sees the
  tracker's reused `TrackList`: after every `update` / `onIdle` the pipeline makes an immutable **`TrackSnapshot`**
  of the *shown* tracks and publishes it in `OverlayState.tracks`. This follows the existing pattern (one new
  immutable `OverlayState` per processed frame); `TrackSnapshot.EMPTY` is shared, so nothing is allocated while
  nobody is tracked. The per-frame objects of the pipeline (state, detections, the snapshot) stay an open item of
  ARCHITECTURE §13, as before.
- **What is drawn:** per shown track the corner brackets at the track box and the head ring at the tracked head
  point. Raw detections are drawn only with `debugMarks` (rectangle and keypoints as today, plus the track id).
  A LOST track is drawn like a CONFIRMED one in M3b (fading is M4).
- **Redraw:** the policy compares the drawn and the new `TrackSnapshot` (count, ids, head points and box edges
  against the deadband). With `debugMarks` the detections are compared as today as well.
- **Idle:** the HUD tick (capture thread, 2 Hz) calls `tracker.onIdle(now − arrival time of the newest processed
  frame)`. A person on a still picture is therefore marked 0.4 to 0.9 s after the last frame (today: at once);
  on a playing video marks appear from the third frame (100 to 150 ms).
- **Reset:** a new expected capture size (rotation, resize) resets the tracker; ids go on.
- A frame the detector did not see (no detector yet, display not sized, copy failed, size mismatch) is **not**
  given to the tracker: no information is not "nobody there".
