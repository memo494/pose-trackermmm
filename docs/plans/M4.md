## Decisions (lead, measured on the reference model)
1. **A tentative track survives `trackTentativeMaxMisses` (1) missed frames** instead of dying on the first.
   Reference model, one person at score 0.4 whose detection is missing in a fraction p of the frames, 20 fps,
   40 seeds: time until the ring is shown, p90, from 1050 to 600 ms at p = 0.4 and from 2650 to 1450 ms at
   p = 0.6; S1, S9 (one-frame and two-frame false positives), S12 and S13 unchanged
   (`docs/plans/M4_sim/tentative.py`). The hit counter is not reset by a miss; a hit resets the miss counter.
2. **A display smoother on top of the tracker:** a OneEuro filter per shown track on the head point (x, y), in
   Img px, cutoff `fc = smoothMinCutoffHz + smoothBeta · |speed| / h` (speed of the filtered derivative in
   px/s, `h` the track's height), derivative cutoff `smoothDerivativeCutoffHz`. Defaults **1.0 Hz, 3.0,
   1.0 Hz**. The box is moved with the smoothed head point (its offsets to the head are kept), so ring and
   brackets stay together. Reference model (`docs/plans/M4_sim/euro.py`), 8 seeds: jitter ratio of a still
   person 0.51 → 0.10 at 20 fps and 0.37 → 0.05 at 30 fps; walking lag +0.37 / +0.50 frame; overshoot after an
   abrupt stop ≤ 0.04 h. `smoothEnabled` (default true) turns it off.
3. **Not in M4a** (M4b): forward prediction, fading LOST tracks, tap-to-lock, the tuning sheet.

# M4b — fading lost tracks and tap-to-lock (plan)

**Branch:** `m4b-fade-lock` · approved in advance (owner rules of 2026-11-15; owner on 2026-11-17: "continue").

## Decision: no forward prediction (lead, measured)
Reference model (`docs/plans/M4_sim/pred.py`, smoothed point + tracker velocity × lead, walk then abrupt stop,
still person, 6 seeds): at 20 fps a lead of 30 / 50 / 70 ms leaves the mean error of a walker unchanged (0.021 h
→ 0.021 / 0.023 / 0.023 h) because the M4a smoother already lags only a third of a frame, but raises the still
jitter ratio from 0.10 to 0.29 / 0.43 / 0.58 and the stop overshoot from 0.026 to 0.056 / 0.081 / 0.112 h
(30 fps alike). Prediction is dropped; ARCHITECTURE §6.4 says so.

## T1 — Fading LOST tracks · M
- `TrackSnapshot` carries per track `lostMs` (0 unless LOST); `ofIdle` adds the idle time since the frame
  (capped at `trackMaxLostMs`), so a paused screen fades too.
- Opacity `alpha = max(fadeMinAlpha, 1 − lostMs / trackMaxLostMs)` (`TrackerConfig.fadeMinAlpha` 0.25, bounds
  0..1, KDoc, test); a pure helper in `core/overlay` (`MarkFade`), tested.
- `RedrawPolicy.tracksChanged` also returns true when a track's alpha changed by at least `fadeStepAlpha`
  (`TrackerConfig`, 0.1) so the fade is drawn in steps without redrawing every frame for nothing.
- `PoseOverlayView` draws a LOST track's ring and brackets with that alpha (reused paints: set the alpha before,
  restore after; no allocation).
- Tests first: snapshot `lostMs` (frame and idle), helper values at 0, half, full and beyond, redraw on a fade
  step, no redraw below it.

## T2 — Tap-to-lock through a handle window · L
As ARCHITECTURE §6.6, simplified for M4b:
- A small touchable handle (`TYPE_APPLICATION_OVERLAY`, `lockHandleSizeDp` 40, at the right screen edge, the
  vertical middle) while a session runs. Tap → **armed**: the main overlay becomes touchable for one tap (the
  HUD shows `tap a person`); the next tap is mapped View → Img (`FitCenter.viewToImgX/Y` of the current
  mapping) and locks the **nearest shown track whose head is within `lockMaxTapDistanceRatio` (1.0) × its
  height** of the tap; none → nothing locked (fail closed). After the tap, or after `lockArmTimeoutMs` (5000),
  the overlay is not touchable again. A tap on the handle while a lock exists unlocks.
- The locked id goes UI thread → capture thread through an `AtomicInteger` in `FramePipeline` (−1 = none); the
  pipeline ends the lock when that id is no longer in the tracker's list (no re-lock in M4b) or on a reset.
  `OverlayState.lockedId` carries it to the view; the view draws the locked track's ring thicker
  (`lockRingStrokeDp` 3) and a second ring (radius × 1.3).
- Pure parts in `core` with JUnit 5 tests: nearest-track choice (`LockPicker`), the arm/timeout state machine
  (`LockArming`, injected clock). App tests (JUnit 4): pipeline ends the lock on removal and reset; redraw on a
  lock change. The window code itself is checked on the phone.
- All new numbers are `TrackerConfig` fields with KDoc, bounds and tests.

# M4c — re-lock the same person by clothing colours (plan)

**Branch:** `m4c-relock` · approved in advance (owner rules of 2026-11-15). **Why:** owner, 2026-11-17 after
testing v0.1.159: "the lock is good, but it does not remember the person after they leave the camera and come
back". Diagnostics of that test (14 min, no crash, 12 rotations): `trackConfirmations/trackBirths` 267/576 = 46 %
(M3: 37 %), result age p50/p95 54/69 ms. The lock leaves no trace in the diagnostics today.

## Design (lead)
- **Signature:** when a lock is set, and on every frame while the locked track is matched (not LOST), the
  pipeline adds the pixels of the locked person's **torso** to an appearance signature: a normalised HSV
  histogram (`relockHueBins` 16 × `relockSatBins` 4 = 64 bins; pixels with value below `relockMinValue` 0.15 or
  saturation below `relockMinSaturation` 0.15 go to one extra "grey" bin set of `relockGreyBins` 4 value bins).
  Torso = the rectangle between the shoulder and hip keypoints when all four are confident, else the box rows
  `0.25..0.6` of its height and the middle 60 % of its width; shrunk by 10 % on every side; at most
  `relockMaxSamplePixels` (2000) pixels on a grid; never the head (the ring is drawn there and is part of the
  capture). The signature is an exponential average (`relockSignatureAlpha` 0.1) so one bad frame does not
  replace it.
- **Waiting:** when the locked track is removed (left, lost too long) the lock becomes **pending** for
  `relockMemoryMs` (60 000) measured on frame timestamps (idle time does not count, like the tracker's rule).
  A reset (rotation, Stop) or a handle tap ends it.
- **Re-lock:** while pending, every track at the moment it becomes CONFIRMED gets one signature from that
  frame and is compared (Bhattacharyya distance). The lock moves to it only if its distance ≤ `relockMaxDistance`
  (0.30) **and** every other shown track's distance is larger by at least `relockMinMargin` (0.10) (fail closed:
  ties and doubt = no re-lock). Otherwise it stays pending until the memory runs out.
- **Logging (also for tuning):** one log line per event: `lock: set id=N`, `lock: cleared (handle|reset|stop)`,
  `lock: pending id=N`, `lock: relocked id=N -> M d=0.18 next=0.41`, `lock: candidate id=M d=… rejected (…)`,
  `lock: expired`. `summary.txt`: `locks`, `lockRelocks`, `lockRelockRejects`, `lockExpired`.
- Display: a pending lock draws nothing (no ghost ring); the re-locked track gets the locked ring at once.
- Privacy: the signature lives only in memory on the capture thread, is a colour histogram (no face, no image),
  and is dropped on Stop, reset, expiry or unlock.

## T1 — Core: `AppearanceSignature` and `RelockMatcher` · M
Pure Kotlin, allocation-free after construction, JUnit 5: histogram from an RGBA array + rectangle (+ grid
step), EMA update, normalisation, Bhattacharyya distance; torso rectangle from a detection (keypoints or box
fallback, shrink, clip to the image, empty → none); the pending/expiry/re-lock decision with an injected time.
Config fields (KDoc, bounds, tests). Tests: identical patches distance ≈ 0; two different colour patches far;
same colours with brightness × 0.8 still close (≤ 0.3); a grey patch; grid sampling caps the pixel count;
margin rule (two similar candidates → no re-lock); expiry; reset.
## T2 — App: wiring, logging, diagnostics · M
`FramePipeline` (capture thread): update the signature while locked and matched; pending on removal; compare
newly confirmed tracks while pending; move `lockedId`; the log lines and the four counters (`CaptureStats`,
`summary.txt`); a handle tap while pending ends it. Tests with fake frames: a person leaves and comes back with
the same colours → re-locked; a differently coloured person → not; two similar people → not; expiry; reset.
## T3 — Docs · S
ARCHITECTURE §6.6 (re-lock as built, limits), §6.7, §7.1, §12, §13, §15 v1.2; TEST_CHECKLIST section M4c.

# M4e — why a moving person gets a new id (diagnostics first) (plan)

**Branch:** `m4e-id-switch-diagnostics` · approved by the owner (2026-11-18, option 1).

## Evidence (v0.1.174 · 3212f34, 2026-11-18 15:19–15:32, 13 min, no crash)
Re-lock: 63 pending → 59 re-locked (d 0.22–0.40), 2 expired, 1 `cleared (no signature)`, `no torso` 2 (was 30);
the d = 1.00 rejects came from a lock with `frames=424`, i.e. very different clothes, not an empty signature.
Owner: re-lock always came back on the same person, even while they kept moving.
**Problem:** in one 3-minute lock the same person got 30+ new ids (one every 2–4 s). Several re-locks happen in the
same millisecond as the pending (e.g. 602 → 603), so the new track was born while the old one was coasting: the person
**was detected but not associated**. captureFps 8.0, infer p50/p95 35.5/54.4 ms, frame p95 67 ms.

## Offline check (docs/plans/M4_sim/walk.py, reference tracker, 20 seeds × 20 s, ±30 % frame jitter)
Id switches per 20 s for one walker: none at 0.5–1 heights/s at 8, 12 or 20 fps (≤ 0.9 with 10 % missed frames);
only fast walking with turns (2 heights/s, turn every 0.5 s) switches (5.8 at 8 fps). **The simulation does not
reproduce the phone**, so the cause is something the simulated detector does not do (score dips to the low stage,
head-keypoint jumps, box-height jumps under occlusion, frozen or repeated frames). We do not guess: measure first.

## Decisions (lead)
1. **Association miss log (core + app):** when a track is born while a CONFIRMED track is LOST and the new detection
   lies within `assocMissNearRatio` (1.0) × the lost track's height of its predicted head, record one line
   `track: miss id=L -> new id=N lostMs=… score=… stage=high|low gateChi2=… iou=… headRatio=… hRatio=… cost=…`
   (gateChi2: the Mahalanobis value against `trackGateChi2`; headRatio: head distance / (assocHeadScaleRatio × h);
   hRatio: detection height / track height). Values are the ones the matcher used, computed only for that pair.
   At most `assocMissLogPerMin` (30) lines per minute; counters always: `assocMisses`, and per reason
   `assocMissGate` (gate failed), `assocMissCost` (cost above the stage limit), `assocMissTaken` (the detection went
   to another track), `assocMissLowBirth` (unmatched but below birth score — counted, no line).
2. No behaviour change in this PR: tracker results must be identical (scenario tests and `MetricsThresholds` untouched;
   a test proves the log does not change any track).
3. No per-frame allocation: fixed-size scratch; the work runs only when a birth happens next to a LOST track.
4. Summary gets the counters; TEST_CHECKLIST says what to send.
5. Review: a birth next to a track that is **not** LOST (matched to a duplicate or neighbour box, or TENTATIVE) or next to a
   track removed this frame gets its own record and counters (`assocBirthNearLive`, `assocBirthAfterRemoval`), with
   `detIou` against the live track's matched box; the cost math is shared with `AssociationCost` (exact equality).
